## Compliant two-column pattern

- Use width `182mm` / `7.16in`.
- Reserve full width for dense multipanel comparisons, large circuit or antenna layouts, or diagrams that cannot remain legible in one column.
- Align panel labels consistently and keep all panel labels at 8pt or larger.
- Use shared axes and shared legends where they reduce visual clutter.
- Keep the final height at or below `220mm` / `8.8in`.
- Start captions with `Fig. N.` and describe panels in reading order.
