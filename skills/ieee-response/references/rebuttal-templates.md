# IEEE Rebuttal Templates

Use these scaffolds after comments have been split into `R1.1`, `R1.2`, and similar IDs. Replace bracketed fields only with author-supplied facts.

## Reply Letter Skeleton

Dear [Editor title and name],

Thank you for the opportunity to revise our manuscript, `[manuscript ID and title]`, for `[IEEE venue]`. We appreciate the editor's guidance and the reviewers' careful comments. Below we provide a point-by-point response and identify where each manuscript change appears in the revised version.

Sincerely,

[Corresponding author name, on behalf of all authors]

## Classification Templates

### Agree and fix

We thank Reviewer [N] for identifying this issue. We have revised [section/figure/table] to [specific change]. The change appears in [location], where we now [author-supplied wording or summary].

### Partially agree

We thank Reviewer [N] for raising this point. We agree that [agreed part] and have revised [location] accordingly. We retain [remaining choice] because [author-supplied evidence or constraint], and we now clarify this rationale in [location].

### Respectfully disagree

We thank Reviewer [N] for the thoughtful comment. We respectfully take a different view because [new evidence, clarified assumption, or manuscript-supported rationale]. To make this clearer, we revised [location] to explain [specific clarification].

### Clarification needed

We thank Reviewer [N] for highlighting a point that needs clarification. Before finalizing this response, the author must confirm [specific missing fact]. `AUTHOR_INPUT_NEEDED: [clear question]`
