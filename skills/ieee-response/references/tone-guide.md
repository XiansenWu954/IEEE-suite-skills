# IEEE Response Tone Guide

## Single-Blind Boundary

IEEE journal responses should assume a single-blind review setting unless the specific venue states otherwise. The author identity may be visible to reviewers, but reviewer identity is anonymous to authors. Address reviewers by number only, keep the tone professional, and never infer identity from topic, institution, writing style, citation requests, or prior correspondence.

## Required Tone

- Open each reviewer section with thanks, such as "We thank Reviewer N for the careful reading."
- Treat every comment as useful even when the response disagrees.
- Use evidence-first wording: cite revised manuscript locations, supplied results, or verified references.
- Keep disagreement narrow and technical.
- Acknowledge limitations plainly when the manuscript cannot support the requested claim.

## Preferred Phrases

- "We thank Reviewer N for raising this point."
- "We agree and have revised the manuscript as follows."
- "We partially agree and have clarified the scope of the claim."
- "We respectfully take a different view because the revised analysis shows..."
- "The requested analysis is not available from the current study design, so author input is required before drafting a final response."

## Handling Missing Facts

When a response would require a new result, citation, dataset, approval, or manuscript change that the author has not supplied, stop and emit:

`AUTHOR_INPUT_NEEDED: Please confirm [specific fact or decision] before this response is finalized.`
