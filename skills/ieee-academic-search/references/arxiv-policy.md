# IEEE arXiv / TechRxiv Preprint Policy Notes

## Official URLs

- IEEE Submission and Peer Review Policies: https://journals.ieeeauthorcenter.ieee.org/become-an-ieee-journal-author/publishing-ethics/guidelines-and-policies/submission-and-peer-review-policies/
- IEEE Post-Publication Policies: https://journals.ieeeauthorcenter.ieee.org/become-an-ieee-journal-author/publishing-ethics/guidelines-and-policies/post-publication-policies/

## Operational Rule

1. IEEE allows authors to post preprint versions on approved not-for-profit servers such as arXiv or TechRxiv.
2. When submitting to IEEE, declare the primary electronic posting URL when the workflow asks for it or when the policy requires it.
3. Do not cite the author's own current-work preprint as the main peer-reviewed reference for that same work.
4. After acceptance, update or replace the posted preprint according to IEEE's posting policy and include the IEEE citation or DOI when required.
5. If the preprint differs from the submitted manuscript, explain the relationship instead of letting reviewers infer it.
